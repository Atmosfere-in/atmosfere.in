# Case study content sheet

Fill one copy of this sheet per project. Keep the headings, replace the
example text. The page layout stays the same for every story; only the
words and photos change. Leave a field blank to drop that part.

Example values below are from Olive Court (hospitality). The same sheet
works for Spaces projects; the "Facts" labels differ slightly (see note).

---

## Project

- **Name:** Olive Court
- **Hub:** Hospitality (or Spaces)
- **Type:** Heritage boutique hotel
  (Spaces examples: Residence, Apartment, Restaurant, Workplace)
- **Location:** Jaipur
- **One-line intro (shown on the hero and the listing, 1–2 sentences):**
  A family haveli turned into an 18-key heritage hotel — by restoring its two courtyards instead of filling them with rooms.

## Facts (shown on the hero)

Hospitality: Property type · Location · Scale · Engagement
- **Scale:** 18 keys
- **Engagement:** Concept → opening

Spaces: Type · Location · Area · Timeline
- **Area:** 5,800 sq ft
- **Timeline:** 11 months

## The story (opening paragraph, 2–4 sentences)

Investors had proposed 40 rooms built over the courtyards. Our feasibility study showed that a smaller hotel, which protected the architecture guests would travel for, would earn more — and keep the family's home intact.

## The concept

- **Title (one line):** Let the haveli be the hotel.
- **Text (2–4 sentences):** Rather than decorating over the building, we planned around what was already there: two courtyards as the hotel's living rooms, a rooftop for dining at sunset, and the old water tank revived as a courtyard pool. New additions are honest and reversible; everything original is restored by hand.

   - Review of the investor's 40-room proposal
2. **Challenge:** A protected building in narrow lanes, rooms of every size, and an offer on the table that would have meant losing the courtyards.
